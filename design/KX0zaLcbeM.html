01 / WHY WE EXIST

A skill. A crew.
A different offer.

PictureThis Production trains young people aged 16 to 29 in sound, cameras, lighting and streaming, then puts them on real crews with a working technician as a mentor.

Every week, churches, concert halls, hotels and conference centres across the GTA pay technicians to run sound, video and lighting. We teach the craft and the conduct — both are the job.

Our vision. A Toronto where young people build their future together, not on the streets, but as a crew: learning skills, earning a living, and looking out for one another.

Our mission. Free, hands-on training and mentorship in live production, on real shows alongside working technicians — a trade, a reference, and a path into paid work.

03 / WHAT COMES NEXT

Pathways,
not promises.

We don’t promise jobs. We promise hours on real gear, a reference from people the industry knows, and a crew that will say your name when a call comes in.

04 / OUR STANDARDS

Mentoring that
takes care.

  • Every mentor passes a Vulnerable Sector Check before working with participants and follows a written code of conduct.
  • Mentors and participants meet in crew spaces, never alone off-site.
  • Participants aged 16 and 17 join with a parent or guardian’s consent.
  • Photos and recordings are used only with consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.
  • The program is free, and stays free. No fees, no gear purchases, no paid upsells.
