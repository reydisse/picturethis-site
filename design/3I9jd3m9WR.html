01 / WHY WE EXIST

A skill, a crew that knows your name, and paid work at the end of it.

PictureThis Production trains young people aged 16 to 29 in sound, cameras, lighting and streaming, then puts them on real crews with a working technician as a mentor. A different offer than the street makes.

Every week, churches, concert halls, hotels and conference centres across the GTA pay technicians to run sound, video and lighting. We teach the craft and the conduct — both are the job.

Our vision — A Toronto where young people build their future together, not on the streets, but as a crew: learning skills, earning a living, and looking out for one another.

Our mission — PictureThis Production gives young people ages 16 to 29 free, hands-on training and mentorship in live production, in sound, cameras, lighting, and streaming, on real shows alongside working technicians. They leave with a trade, a reference, and a path into paid work.

02 / PROGRAMS

Training. Mentoring. Pathways. Production management. Technical management.

Eight weeks, taken together.
Everyone starts at week 1. Nobody is behind.

01

Cables, connectors and signal flow

Coil, tape and test every cable type we own. Follow a signal from microphone to speaker and find the break we hid in it.

02

Digital consoles: the Wing and X32

Build a show file from empty: inputs, gains, EQ, routing, scenes. The two consoles most GTA rooms actually run.

03

Mixing a live band

Soundcheck a real band at Faithfire. Vocals first, then everything else. Learn what to do when it feeds back anyway.

04

Cameras and video switching (ATEM)

Frame, focus and follow. Then sit in the director’s chair and call a two-camera rehearsal on comms.

05

Lighting: rigging safely, running a desk

Hang, focus and cable a rig without hurting anyone. Program cues and run a service from the lighting desk.

06

Streaming, lyrics and media

Build a full ProPresenter service file: lyrics, scripture, sermon slides, stream output. Advance it live, on the director’s call.

07

Show calling, comms and event etiquette

Call a show start to finish. Comms discipline, dress codes, client manners — the conduct half of the job.

08

Capstone: crew a real production

A full event, crewed entirely by the cohort, mentors on comms only if needed. This is the reference you graduate with.

Supervised, but real. From week three you are on a real crew at Faithfire Production or a partner venue. Training runs at 139 Millwick Dr and partner venues across the GTA.

One-to-one mentoring. Monthly check-ins with a working technician. All mentors are police-checked and meet participants in crew spaces, never alone off-site.

Our standards

  • Every mentor passes a Vulnerable Sector Check before working with participants and follows a written code of conduct.
  • Mentors and participants meet in crew spaces, never alone off-site.
  • Participants aged 16 and 17 join with a parent or guardian's consent.
  • Photos and recordings of participants are used only with consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.
  • The program is free, and it stays free. No fees, no gear purchases, no paid upsells.

Run the whole show. Production Management. The other half of putting on a show: advancing the event, building the run sheet and call sheets, managing the budget, and calling the show on the day. Participants who work well on a crew learn to run the production, not just handle one role in it.

Lead the department. Technical Management. For those who go on to lead a department: planning the rig, choosing and maintaining the gear, signing off on safety, and teaching the next cohort. It's the top of the pathway, the people who spec out the room and are accountable for it.

04 / GET INVOLVED

Opportunity is a team effort.

01

Mentor

FOH engineers, video directors, camera ops, lighting and streaming techs with working credits. Two evenings a month, or two Sundays on crew, for eight weeks. We arrange and cover the police check.

02

Donate

$40: course materials. $90: TTC fares for a month. $250: cables, repairs and replacements for the shared gear library. Gear donations welcome too — we collect anywhere in the GTA.

03

Partner

Host a training Saturday, or take trainees onto your event crew. You get a trained, supervised crew that knows your room — and first call on graduates when you are hiring.

  • $40 covers one participant's course materials for the full eight weeks.
  • $90 covers a term of transit stipends so a participant can actually get to training.
  • $250 goes to the gear library: the shared kit participants learn on and borrow for first calls.
