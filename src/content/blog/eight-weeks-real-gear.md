**Every Saturday at 10 AM, twenty young people walk into a working production space, and by 2 PM, they've done a real part of the job. No classroom theatre. The gear is the same gear our crews use on shows that week.**

Week 1 is all about cables, connectors, and signal flow. You coil, tape, and test every type of cable we own, then trace a signal from a microphone to a speaker and find the break we've hidden in it. Week 2 puts you on digital consoles, the Wing and the X32, building a show file from scratch: inputs, gains, EQ, routing, and scenes.

Week 3, you mix a live band at Faithfire, starting with the vocals, then bringing in everything else. You'll also learn what to do when feedback kicks in anyway. Week 4 is all about cameras and video switching: frame, focus, and follow, then take the director's chair and call a two-camera rehearsal over comms.

Week 5 covers lighting: hang, focus, and cable a rig safely, then program cues and run a service from the desk. Week 6 is streaming, lyrics, and media: build a complete service file and advance it live at the director's cue. Week 7 covers show calling, comms, and event etiquette, the conduct side of the job, because how you work with a crew is part of the craft.

Week 8 is the big finale. A full event, run entirely by the cohort, with mentors on comms only if needed. That's the show you'll graduate with as your reference.

Everyone starts together in week 1, and nobody falls behind. The course is free, all the gear is provided, and it takes place at Faithfire Production, 139 Millwick Dr, and partner venues across the GTA. The next cohort starts in January, with twenty places for young people ages 16 to 29. No experience needed. Just bring yourself.
