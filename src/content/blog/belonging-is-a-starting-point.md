# Belonging is a starting point

*Why skills and trusted relationships belong in the conversation about youth violence prevention.*

A young person deserves more than a warning about what not to do. They deserve meaningful opportunities, people who show up and somewhere to contribute.

Public Safety Canada identifies limited community attachment, poor educational or employment prospects, and a need for recognition and belonging among factors associated with youth gang involvement. It also makes an essential point: the vast majority of young people never join gangs or become involved in crime. Risk factors are not a verdict on an individual. [1]

## What a crew can offer

The same research describes positive relationships with mentors and peers, and supportive community environments, as protective factors. PictureThis's eight-week AV course offers a practical setting for learning together: hands-on training, supervised crew experience and one-to-one mentoring. That is a reason to explore its role in prevention, not evidence that this program has already reduced crime. [1]

## Training is one part of the picture

For someone sleeping rough, navigating court or trying to leave a harmful situation, an AV course alone is not enough. A responsible expansion would build relationships with qualified housing, mental-health, legal and youth-support services, with consent-led referrals and clear safeguarding responsibilities. Those arrangements need to be established before they are offered.

Canada's Youth Gang Prevention Fund emphasises targeted, evidence-based interventions addressing risk and protective factors. This is useful context for program design; it is not a funding award or endorsement of PictureThis. [2]

## Come and be part of it

Belonging is not a slogan here. It is a Saturday morning, a crew that knows your name, and work that matters. If you are 16 to 29 and want in, or you work with young people and want to talk, we would like to meet you. The next cohort starts in January, and the Get Involved page is where it begins.
