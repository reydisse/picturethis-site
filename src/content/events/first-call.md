First Call is a free, hands-on introduction to live production for people who have never had a way in, run by First Call Collective, a resident-led group in North York. Register once for all four Saturdays.

- **June 12:** How a show comes together. Cables, signal flow, and what every crew role actually does.
- **June 19:** Sound basics. Hands-on time on a real mixing console.
- **June 26:** Cameras and streaming basics. Framing, switching, and how a livestream is built.
- **July 3:** Crew roles and show etiquette, ending with a closing showcase crewed entirely by the participants.

Participants aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian consent form, including photo consent, before the first session.
