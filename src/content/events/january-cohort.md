The next PictureThis cohort: eight Saturdays, taken together. Everyone starts at week 1, and nobody is behind.

You'll train on the consoles, cameras and lighting desks that GTA rooms actually run, and from week three you'll crew real events, supervised but real. Week 8 is a full production crewed by the cohort.

It's free. There are no fees, no gear to buy and no paid upsells. [See the full eight-week course](/programs/).

Please check with the team before travelling.
