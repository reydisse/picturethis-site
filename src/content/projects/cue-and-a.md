# Cue & A

Cue & A is the training event Faithfire Production hosts for the media teams and churches around us: a day built around the questions crews actually ask when the show is on the line. How do you call it, cue it, recover when it goes sideways?

It is the PictureThis model pointed outward. The same crew that trains on Sunday services opened the room to other teams, taught what we know, and learned from how they run theirs. The first edition was a hit, and it is the kind of room we want to keep building: working technicians teaching, crews learning from each other, nobody performing expertise they do not have.
