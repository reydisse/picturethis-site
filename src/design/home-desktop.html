Free training + mentoring · Greater Toronto Area

Free training. Real shows. A real path into the industry.

PictureThis Production opens the door to live production careers. We offer free, hands-on training and mentorship in sound, cameras, lighting, and streaming, learned on real shows beside working technicians. Everyone who trains with us leaves with a trade, a reference, and a path into paid work.

Who this is for

Our cohorts are built for people the industry rarely reaches: young people ages 16 to 29 from under-resourced neighbourhoods, people rebuilding after the justice system or in recovery, women, families, and people with disabilities, with outreach in Black, Ghanaian, and French-speaking communities.

The eight-week course

Hands on the gear from the first morning.

See the full programme
01

Cables, connectors and signal flow

Coil, tape and test every cable type we own. Follow a signal from microphone to speaker and find the break we hid in it.

02

Digital consoles: the Wing and X32

Build a show file from empty: inputs, gains, EQ, routing, scenes. The two consoles most GTA rooms actually run.

03

Mixing a live band

Soundcheck a real band at Faithfire. Vocals first, then everything else. Learn what to do when it feeds back anyway.

04

Cameras and video switching (ATEM)

Frame, focus and follow. Then sit in the director’s chair and call a two-camera rehearsal on comms.

05

Lighting: rigging safely, running a desk

Hang, focus and cable a rig without hurting anyone. Program cues and run a service from the lighting desk.

06

Streaming, lyrics and media

Build a full ProPresenter service file: lyrics, scripture, sermon slides, stream output. Advance it live, on the director’s call.

07

Show calling, comms and event etiquette

Call a show start to finish. Comms discipline, dress codes, client manners — the conduct half of the job.

08

Capstone: crew a real production

A full event, crewed entirely by the cohort, mentors on comms only if needed. This is the reference you graduate with.
