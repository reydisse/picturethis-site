Free training. Real shows. A real path into the industry.

PictureThis Production opens the door to live production careers. We offer free, hands-on training and mentorship in sound, cameras, lighting, and streaming, learned on real shows beside working technicians. Everyone who trains with us leaves with a trade, a reference, and a path into paid work.

Participants pay nothing, ever. Gear is provided.

Who this is for

Our cohorts are built for people the industry rarely reaches: young people ages 16 to 29 from under-resourced neighbourhoods, people rebuilding after the justice system or in recovery, women, families, and people with disabilities, with outreach in Black, Ghanaian, and French-speaking communities.

  • Young people, 16 to 29.
  • People rebuilding.
  • People in recovery.
  • Women.
  • Children and families.
  • People with disabilities.
  • Our communities.

One cohort, every community. The January cohort is open to anyone on this list. If you see yourself here, apply.

How it works

A skill, a crew that knows your name, and a path into paid work.

01

Train

Eight Saturdays on sound, cameras, lighting and streaming. Hands on the gear from the first morning.

02

Crew

From week three you are on a real crew at Faithfire Production or a partner venue — supervised, but real.

03

Mentor

Monthly one-to-one check-ins with a working technician, and a reference from people the industry knows.

The eight-week course

Hands on the gear from the first morning.

  • 01Cables, connectors and signal flow
  • 02Digital consoles: the Wing and X32
  • 03Mixing a live band
  • 04Cameras and video switching (ATEM)
  • 05Lighting: rigging safely, running a desk
  • 06Streaming, lyrics and media
  • 07Show calling, comms and event etiquette
  • 08Capstone: crew a real production
